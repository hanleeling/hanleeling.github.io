## Dissertation

My dissertation investigates the historical phonology of the Loushao varieties of Xiang Chinese, with particular attention to consonant strengthening and its interaction with high-vowel frication and related sound changes. Through comparative reconstruction and cross-dialectal analysis, I examine the phonological conditions and mechanisms underlying these developments and their implications for the historical relationships among Loushao varieties.

## Northern Jê

My research outside Chinese includes comparative work on the Jê languages of Brazil. In ongoing work on Northern Jê, I use comparative reconstruction to investigate the history of coronal obstruents and the development of consonant systems.
